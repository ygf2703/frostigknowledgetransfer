- Compare the output with the original brief: was every requirement met, in the requested format and language?

Outputs:
- A verdict line.
- A findings table: location, label, what is wrong, proposed fix.
- A list of what was checked and what could not be checked.

- Review only. Do not rewrite the work or fix it silently - return findings to the author or the user.

- A verdict and a one-paragraph summary.
- Findings grouped by severity, each with a fix.
- A list of what you could not check (no way to run the code, missing context).

- Do not claim a bug without a scenario that triggers it.
